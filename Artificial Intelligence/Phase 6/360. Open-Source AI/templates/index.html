API-Based AI vs Local AI

Understand where the model actually runs.

☁️ API-Based AI

Your Application ↓ Internet ↓ Remote AI Server ↓ Response
  • Model runs remotely
  • Usually requires internet
  • No large model download
  • Provider manages hardware
  • API limits may apply

💻 Local AI

Your Application ↓ Local Model ↓ Your CPU/GPU ↓ Response
  • Model runs on your machine
  • Can work offline after download
  • Model files are stored locally
  • You manage hardware
  • RAM/VRAM limits matter
